# read.me

What is Budgie, you might ask? Budgie is basically Mate with a theme. Mate is just XFCE with plugins, and XFCE is simply advanced Xorg (or Wayland). Xorg (or Wayland), is only an easier Linux graphics stack and a shiny alternative to the Linux console. So, in the end, Budgie, GNOME, KDE, and all that eye-catching stuff is really just a funny-looking terminal.

You might say that UNIX UIs are just a sparkling illusion, but be reminded that Windows users use their file explorer as a window manager.

So, are all UIs a big lie? Well, your eyes are just photon detectors, and your brain is just a big GPU computing the informations it receives to make it fit your beliefs.

In conclusion, before judging or even thinking something is right or wrong, remind yourself that everything is just a big theater play animated by yourself, trying to make sense of all the mess you receive.
